Chain Rule:
[f(g(x))]' = f'(g(x))·g'(x)
Even Function:
f(-x) = f(x) → f' is odd
Odd Function:
f(-x) = -f(x) → f' is even
[f(x)]ⁿ' = n[f(x)]ⁿ⁻¹·f'(x)
f(g(x))' = f'(g(x))·g'(x)
√f(x)' = f'(x)/(2√f(x))
